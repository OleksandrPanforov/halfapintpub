## ABOUT ME

And why I write here.

As it happened, I started playing tabletop roleplaying games as a game master. I eventually got to play in other people's games, too, but the desire to run games never went away. Time passed, experience accumulated, and countless debates about systems, rules, and approaches carried on, so I decided it was time to write some of it down. That way I wouldn't have to repeat myself every time, and more tabletop RPG content in Ukrainian can only benefit the community.

I run ongoing campaigns, have hosted large games, and created original story arcs and strange one-shots.

I enjoy D&D, Pathfinder, Fallout P&P, and Call of Cthulhu above all.

My published adventures on [DMsGuild](https://www.dmsguild.com/browse.php?keywords=Oleksandr+Panforov&x=0&y=0&author=&artist=&pfrom=&pto=)

Spoiler: they're free.
